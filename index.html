Darkroom support

Darkroom keeps your private photos, videos and notes locked on your iPhone and iPad. Only the pattern you draw can open them.

Contact us

Email business@inertiaprojects.info. It helps to include your app version (in the app, Settings › App settings › About) and your iPhone model. Please never send us your pattern or recovery phrase.

I forgot my pattern

We can't reset it, and neither can Apple. Darkroom never saves your pattern, which is what keeps your photos private. If you set up a recovery phrase (Settings › Recovery phrase), tap “Use recovery phrase” on the lock screen to get back in.

Moving to a new iPhone

If iCloud backup is on, sign in to the same Apple Account on your new iPhone, open Darkroom and draw your pattern on the “Restore your vault” screen. Small previews show up first, and full-size photos keep downloading in the background. Any decoy vaults stay in iCloud until you draw their pattern.

Getting photos out

Select the photos you want, then tap Export and choose Save to Photos or Save to Files. They keep their full quality and the date they were taken.

Subscriptions

If Pro ends, you can still view, export and back up everything already in your vault. To manage or cancel, open iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions. To restore a purchase, open Darkroom and go to Settings › App settings › Restore purchases.
