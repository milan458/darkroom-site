Darkroom support

Darkroom hides photos, videos and notes in encrypted vaults on your iPhone and iPad, opened with a pattern of bolts only you know.

Contact

Email business@inertiaprojects.info. Please include your app version (Settings › Support in the app) and your iPhone model. Never send us your bolt pattern or passphrase.

I forgot my bolt pattern

Nobody can reset it: not us, not Apple. Darkroom never stores your pattern, and your files are encrypted with a key that only your pattern (or your passphrase, if you added one) can unlock. If you set a passphrase in Settings › The door, tap “Use passphrase” on the lock screen.

Moving to a new iPhone

With iCloud backup on, sign in to the same Apple Account on the new iPhone, open Darkroom and draw your pattern on the Restore screen. Thumbnails arrive first; originals follow in the background. Other rooms stay in iCloud until you draw their patterns.

Getting photos out

Select frames, then Export › Save to Photos or Save to Files. Originals keep their full quality and the date they were taken.

Subscriptions

If Pro ends, you keep full access to view, export and back up everything already in your vault. Manage or cancel in iOS Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions. To restore a purchase, open Settings › Pro › Restore purchases.
