Privacy policy

Darkroom: Photo Vault for iPhone and iPad. Effective 20 October 2026.

The short version

We don't collect any data. Darkroom has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your photos, videos and notes are encrypted on your device, and nobody but you can read them: not us, not Apple.

What stays on your device

Everything you put in Darkroom is encrypted on your iPhone or iPad with AES-256-GCM before it is saved. Names, dates, places and notes are encrypted too. Your bolt pattern and passphrase are never stored; they are turned into a key on the device with Argon2id.

iCloud backup

If you turn on iCloud backup, Darkroom stores encrypted copies in the private CloudKit database of your own Apple Account. Only encrypted data leaves your device. It counts toward your iCloud storage. We have no access to it, and Apple cannot decrypt it. You can turn backup off in Settings at any time.

Shared vaults

If you share a vault, the person you invite gets access to that one vault through iCloud sharing. It is protected by a passphrase you give them yourself.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details and no personal information from them.

Crash reports

Darkroom contains no crash reporter or analytics of its own. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may pass us anonymous crash reports, which never include your photos, videos or notes.

Permissions

Darkroom asks for the camera, microphone and Photos only when you use those features, and Face ID only to open your vault. Location data stays inside your encrypted photos.

Children

Darkroom does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date. Questions: business@inertiaprojects.info.
